Q-012 · added November 6, 2026

How much federal funding is documented?

Documented: HUD’s FY2026 allocation file and Tucson’s FY2027/PY2026 Annual Action Plan agree on four allocations:

  • Community Development Block Grant (CDBG): $4,962,047 for eligible community-development work, including housing.
  • HOME: $3,061,840.81 for the Tucson/Pima County consortium.
  • ESG: $448,998 for Tucson’s Emergency Solutions Grant.
  • HOPWA: $1,140,597 for housing assistance and services for low-income people with HIV/AIDS and their families.

Together, the allocations total $9,613,482.81. The City separately forecasts $200,000 in CDBG income and $500,000 in HOME income. Each program has its own eligible uses and service area.

Unknown: How much has been received, committed or spent is not established by these allocations and forecasts.

Next: Match receipts and spending to completed activities and aggregate household counts for the same reporting period.

Source date: HUD FY2026 allocations; City plan May 2026, covering July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027. [1, 2]

Q-013 · added November 6, 2026

What do the two Craycroft financing records mean?

Documented: The records appear to concern the same financing. The Arizona Finance Authority lists a $10 million volume-cap allocation, approved February 9 and expiring December 15, 2026. The November 4 Council agenda proposed up to $10 million in project revenue bonds for the same project. They should not be added as separate funding.

Unknown: The adopted action, bond closing and proceeds received remain unverified. Blank closing fields in the older AFA tracker do not establish current status.

Next: Check the adopted action, closing documents and use of proceeds.

Source date: AFA tracker October 7, 2026; Council agenda November 4, 2026. [3, 4]

The separately documented $500,000 Pima County preservation award is distinct from these bond-financing records.

Q-014 · added November 6, 2026

How much CoC funding was requested?

Documented: TPCH submitted a $22,827,346 total FY2026 Continuum of Care request on September 29, 2026. It includes renewals and other categories, not just new funding. The submission is not a final HUD award. [12, 13]

Next: Compare final awards with the request. HUD’s November 3 notice says awards are required by December 1, 2026. [14]

Research standards

Plans, awards, commitments, expenditures, restricted balances, completed housing, and actual vacancies are separate measures. Published figures have limited reporting periods and may rely on historical agency statements. Questions are not findings of fraud or noncompliance.
