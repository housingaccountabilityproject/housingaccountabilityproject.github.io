Home energy assistance

Housing Help: Weatherization and home energy upgrades

Eligible TEP customers may qualify for no-cost energy-efficiency upgrades, subject to available funding. In Tucson and South Tucson, call AllThrive 365 at 520-485-4985. For other Pima County addresses, check the TEP weatherization page or call Pima County Home Repair Assistance at 520-724-2461 to confirm the right provider, eligibility and availability.

Independent-living support

Greater Vail: home-delivered meals

The Greater Vail Homebound Meal Program serves homebound adults age 60 and older within its service area in the Vail School District. For meal delivery inquiries or to check whether your address is served, call 520-338-2610 or email foodbank@resourcesvail.org.
