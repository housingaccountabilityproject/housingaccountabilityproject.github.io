Goodyear plans home repairs and legal help for renters

Goodyear’s 2026–27 action plan lists $323,561 for repairs to owner-occupied homes, $62,268 for legal services for renters facing unlawful eviction, and $96,457 for administration. Those three lines total $482,286. The plan covers July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. These figures are planned uses; they do not show how much was paid, whether services have started, or how many households got help. Read the City’s project breakdown.

Sources and method

HAP matched Arizona grantee names and HUD grantee keys to the regular CDBG column in the original annual formula allocation workbooks. The checked set contains 19 named places or grantees, 133 place/year checks and 101 direct HUD allocations. It is not an inventory of all CDBG-supported activity. Allocations are not adjusted for inflation, and do not include separately reported CDBG-CV or disaster funds.

Download the 133 checked source rows (CSV). Each positive row has a source workbook, sheet, cell and exact amount. A blank amount represents no separate row; never replace it with zero.
