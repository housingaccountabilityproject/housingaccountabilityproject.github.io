  1. Pima supportive-services budget

    Source check · November 19, 2026 · Tucson & Pima County

    Pima County approved an amendment to the same Tucson Choice Neighborhoods Supportive Services award, reducing the approved budget from $202,734 to $85,205.60 and revising the term to October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027.

  2. State shelter reservations

    Source check · November 19, 2026 · Arizona statewide reference

    Arizona’s Housing Solutions list identifies $9 million in second-round reservations for shelter and transitional-housing operations. The $16.2 million shown on the combined list includes both rounds. In the second round, the listed projects total $1.14 million in Pima County and about $4.10 million in Maricopa County.

    Read full article & sources: State shelter reservations

  3. Tucson and Phoenix rent benchmarks

    Source check · November 19, 2026 · Arizona statewide reference

    HUD’s FY2027 two-bedroom Fair Market Rent is $1,405 for the Tucson area and $1,734 for the Phoenix–Mesa–Chandler area, effective October 1, 2026.

    Read full article & sources: Tucson and Phoenix rent benchmarks

  4. Pima raises heat-relief budget line

    Source check · November 19, 2026 · Tucson & Pima County

    On November 10, Pima County approved an amendment increasing the Heat Relief Initiative Replacement line from $72,000 to $96,022.43 under its utility program with Wildfire. The $24,022.43 increase is a change to one County budget line, separate from Phoenix’s Wildfire amendment.

    Read full article & sources: Pima raises heat-relief budget line

  5. Four Pima housing proposals appear on state reservation lists

    Source check · November 19, 2026 · Tucson & Pima County

    Arizona’s tax-credit list identifies 67 proposed tax-credit apartments at El Rincon, 44 at Stone & Speedway Phase I, 80 at Drexel Commons and 63 at Mountain Trace Terrace. The state separately lists National Housing Trust Fund reservations of $1,918,471 for Drexel Commons and $1.3 million for Stone & Speedway.

    Read full article & sources: Four Pima housing proposals appear on state reservation lists
