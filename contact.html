Privacy and Important Limitations

Please don't send sensitive personal information through ordinary email. Avoid transmitting Social Security numbers, medical records, identification documents, case files, or confidential housing records without discussing a suitable secure method first.

The project conducts independent research and cannot process housing applications, provide emergency shelter, guarantee individual assistance, or offer legal representation. Messages are reviewed as capacity permits; a response is not guaranteed. Sending information does not authorize its public release.

About This Initiative

The Housing Accountability Project is an independent, nonpartisan public-interest research initiative. It is not affiliated with HUD, the City of Tucson, Pima County, or a government auditing authority.

Learn more in our Research Library, Public Evidence Index, and Research Findings.
