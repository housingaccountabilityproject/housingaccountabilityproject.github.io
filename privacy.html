Visitor Analytics and Privacy

Visitor Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter to measure visits and referring links, including campaign parameters. Analytics include session estimates, browser and operating system, screen size, country, and region in the United States, Russia, and China.

The dashboard is restricted to logged-in users. Individual-pageview export storage and language collection are disabled; automatic data deletion is not configured.

These settings were checked on November 6, 2026. They describe the configuration, not a guarantee that every visit is counted. Browsers and blockers can prevent measurement. See GoatCounter’s privacy information.

Website Search

The website search runs in your browser using a copy of the public search index. The search feature does not send your search terms to a server, add them to the page address, or save them in browser storage. Opening a result is a normal page visit and may be counted by GoatCounter.

People in the Research

Research questions seek aggregate counts and public or appropriately redacted records, not residents’ identities or individual case records. Read the resident privacy standard.
