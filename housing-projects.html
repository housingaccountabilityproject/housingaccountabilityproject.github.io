Amazon Flats

59 apartments described

The City announced the start of construction in April 2026. An earlier environmental notice proposed 39 project-based vouchers and identified two HOME-designated apartments within the 59 planned apartments.

Source: City notices cited in F-002

Important limit: The City projected fall 2027 completion; a projection is not confirmation of completion, executed contracts, vacancies or current applications. Do not add 59, 39 and 2 as distinct apartment counts.

Craycroft Towers and Lee Street Duplexes

74 + 8 existing apartments; 82 PBVs allocated

City notices describe 74 Craycroft and eight Lee Street apartments. The 82 Section 8 project-based vouchers concern the existing units, with RAD and Section 18 classifications also described for Craycroft.

Source: City notice cited in F-003

Research standards

Plans, awards, commitments, expenditures, restricted balances, completed housing, and actual vacancies are separate measures. Published figures have limited reporting periods and may rely on historical agency statements. Questions are not findings of fraud or noncompliance.

Detailed source records remain in the Public Evidence Index. Corrections and additional official records can be shared through Contact.
