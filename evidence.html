What this index does

This is a curated index of selected Tucson City housing records, relevant Continuum of Care material, and internal analysis from the Master Research Workbook (v12.6) and the Accountability Audit (v1.7). It is not a complete Pima County-wide source inventory, a completed audit, or a representation of currently available housing vacancies.

How to read this page: A verified City web notice establishes what that notice says, not that a proposed action was implemented. A linked PDF is not necessarily page-verified. Workbook calculations are identified as internal. Agency records, project calculations, and outstanding interpretations must be kept separate. This page was reviewed against selected records through October 31, 2026; it does not check updates automatically.

Financial classification: no invalid grand total

Historical reported activity

PY2024 CAPER resources and expenditures are historical and subject to program-specific definitions. A subtraction is not an available-cash certificate.

Planning and proposed funding

FY2027/PY2026 AAP amounts are planned allocation categories, not certified HUD awards. FY2028 P-CHIP solicitation estimates belong to a different period and cannot be added to FY2027 resources.

Transfers and restricted balances

Reprogramming shifts categories; match-credit carryover is not cash. HOME recapture, program income, and HCV reserves have separate legal and accounting restrictions.

Units versus outcomes

Proposed PBVs, awarded projects, construction starts, completed units, occupied units, and available units are distinct measures. Shelter service volume is not permanent housing placement.

Methodology, source quality and corrections

Three independent checks

Source: document title, agency, date, version, URL, page. Extraction: quote or quantitative input and reproducible arithmetic. Interpretation: comparable periods, missing context and fair alternative explanations.

Publication boundary

Public summaries are drawn from public records. Internal workbooks include legacy private case material and are not being posted or linked. Medical, personal and confidential correspondence is excluded.

Correction process

Material corrections should retain the question ID and publicly identify affected figures, old and new wording, supporting source, reason, and revision date. Old conclusions must not simply disappear.

Review limitations

This release checks selected source links and workbook controls, not every cell of the 312-sheet master workbook or every original PDF page. Some council and PDF endpoints block automated retrieval.
