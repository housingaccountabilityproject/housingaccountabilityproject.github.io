Units Versus Outcomes

Proposed PBVs, awarded projects, construction starts, completed units, occupied units, and available units are distinct measures. Shelter service volume is not permanent housing placement.

Methodology, Source Quality and Corrections

Three Independent Checks

Source: document title, agency, date, version, URL, page. Extraction: quote or quantitative input and reproducible arithmetic. Interpretation: comparable periods, missing context and fair alternative explanations.

Publication Boundary

Public summaries are drawn from public records. Internal workbooks include legacy private case material and are not being posted or linked. Medical, personal and confidential correspondence is excluded.

Correction Process

Material corrections should retain the question ID and publicly identify affected figures, old and new wording, supporting source, reason, and revision date. Old conclusions must not simply disappear.

Review Limitations

This release checks selected source links and workbook controls, not every cell of the full master workbook or every original PDF page. Some council and PDF endpoints block automated retrieval.
