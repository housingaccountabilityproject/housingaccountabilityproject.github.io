What This Index Does

This is a curated index of selected Tucson City and Pima County housing records, relevant Continuum of Care material, and internal analysis from the Master Research Workbook (v12.6) and the Accountability Audit (v1.7). It is not a complete Pima County-wide source inventory, a completed audit, or a representation of currently available housing vacancies.

How to read this page: A verified City web notice establishes what that notice says, not that a proposed action was implemented. A linked PDF is not necessarily page-verified. Workbook calculations are identified as internal. Agency records, project calculations, and outstanding interpretations must be kept separate. The original index covered selected records through October 31, 2026. Q-007 was corrected November 6, 2026 using the City CAPER and OMB reporting guidance; other records retain their own source dates and verification limits. Research Questions — Source-to-Conclusion Crosswalk

Resident privacy: Resident outcomes are researched through aggregate counts, reporting periods, inclusion rules and denominators. We do not seek resident names, occupied-unit identifiers, tenant rosters or individual case files. Use public or appropriately redacted records and combine or suppress small groups whenever totals or linked information could identify someone. Project and agency identities remain part of public accountability.
