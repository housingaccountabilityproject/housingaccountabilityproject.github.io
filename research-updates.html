INDEPENDENT PUBLIC RESEARCH · TUCSON, ARIZONA

Research updates and evidence notes

Initial release: November 2, 2026 · CDBG clarification: November 3, 2026 · Reporting periods and source dates are noted for each finding.

These notes summarize public records. Reported resources, project commitments, expenditures, and available housing units are different measures. They must not be treated as interchangeable.

1. PY2024 federal program financial reporting

The City of Tucson's PY2024 CAPER CR-15 table reports the following program resources available and expenditures. These are historical reporting figures, not unrestricted funds currently available for new assistance.

2. HOME-assisted housing activity classifications

The PY2024 CAPER's list of four open HOME activities identifies 7 HOME-assisted rental units (2 at Rincon Manor and 5 at Sleepy Hollow Estates) and 18 HOME-assisted homeownership units (7 at Corazon del Pueblo and 11 at Rodeo Addition). These are HOME-assisted unit counts, not total project units.

Availability limitation: These records do not establish that any of the units are currently vacant, completed, accepting applications, or available through project-based vouchers.

Source: City of Tucson, PY2024 CAPER, printed page 37 (PDF page 40), HOME activities. City HCD reports directory. Last reviewed November 2, 2026.

3. CDBG funding reprogramming — approval reported

An October 9, 2026, update from Tucson's Ward 6 office reports that Mayor and Council approved an approximately $1.5 million CDBG adjustment on October 6, shifting funds toward affordable rental and homeowner housing activities. This is an account of a reported policy approval, not confirmation that funds have been drawn or spent.

Source: Ward 6 October 9, 2026, update. Further verification needed: official action record, adopted amendment, and financial-system entries. Last reviewed November 2, 2026.

Open research questions

The following are unresolved and are not allegations of misconduct.

  • How does the PY2024 CAPER CR-15 CDBG expenditure total ($4,717,936) reconcile to the narrative CDBG amount ($3,433,346.66) and the separately stated CDBG-CV amount ($339,616.74)? Subtracting both narrative figures from the table amount produces $944,972.60 in arithmetic difference, but the categories may not use equivalent accounting scopes. Separately, a $1,284,589.71 comparison in the research workbook uses a purported corrected PR-26 amount ($4,717,936.37) and only the CDBG narrative. The original Tucson PR-26 report was not located in the examined records, so the latter figure is not independently source-verified. Neither difference establishes missing funds. See Finding F-007 and Question Q-007.
  • What do the relevant IDIS activity-level commitments and draw records establish?
  • What are the current contracted, occupied, and vacant PBV counts, as of a specific date?

Methodology and corrections

We distinguish direct official records, official summaries, secondary accounts, and unresolved questions. Publication is not a determination of legal liability or financial impropriety. If an underlying document or calculation is corrected, this page will be updated with the date and nature of the change.
