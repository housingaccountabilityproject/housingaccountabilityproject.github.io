What the records establish

Phoenix’s draft FY2025–26 CAPER reports 25th @ Bell completed with 53 apartments.

Separate City and State HOME activities describe the same physical property; their assisted-unit designations must be matched before counting unique homes.

What still needs evidence

Executed assistance agreements and unit schedules are needed to establish designation overlap, applicable restrictions and the changed ARPA terms.

Project-specific disbursement records and current occupancy evidence remain unverified.

04 / What remains unknown?

Keep the gaps
in plain sight.

These are limits of the reviewed evidence, not findings of misuse or noncompliance. The next step is to review executed agreements, amendments and unit schedules.

?

Which designated units overlap?

City and State HOME schedules have not been matched unit by unit. HOME and ARPA set-asides are also not established as separate apartments.

?

When do the restrictions end?

Executed affordability start dates, terms and expiry remain unknown. A dated authorization is not a verified end date.

?

Who currently owns the property?

Current deed ownership and the parcel/legal description have not been verified.

?

What explains the changed ARPA terms?

The 2023 authorization and 2025 report give different unit and income statements. An executed amendment has not been verified.

?

What was actually paid?

City cash disbursements, loan balances, total development cost and a complete financing stack are not established.

?

What is the current occupancy status?

The City draft CAPER is not a verified current occupancy record or an inspected certificate of occupancy.
