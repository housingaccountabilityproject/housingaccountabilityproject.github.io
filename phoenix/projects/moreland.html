The Moreland

Two planned phases. Separate financing records. Follow the dates, financial stages and questions that remain open.

Original research snapshot: November 8, 2026. Later findings retain their own source dates.

What the records establish

The Moreland is planned as two phases totaling 237 apartments: 132 in Phase I and 105 in Phase II.

At the June 17, 2026 Council action, the staff report described Phase I as under construction and forecast completion in February 2028.

Phoenix IDA reports $43.1 million of Moreland I financing closed in December 2025; the preceding Council approval allowed up to $50 million.

What still needs evidence

Match project-specific disbursements to the funder, program, phase and period before calculating any spending per apartment.

Executed assistance agreements, fund allocations and voucher records are needed before treating authorizations as paid or contracted assistance.

The combined 237 apartments describe planned scope across two phases. An older summary labels Phase I with 237; the published research says not to use that as completed Phase I units. Phase I: the funding record

The reported closing and the earlier approval ceiling describe financing stages. Neither is verified City cash spending or the total development cost.

Issuer-reported closed conduit financing; not City spending

Phase II: the funding record
