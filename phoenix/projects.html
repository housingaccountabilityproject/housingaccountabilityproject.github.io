Place

Homes

Funding record
Phase I: $43.1m reported closing, December 2025. Phase II: up to $10m authorized, June 17, 2026.

Read the published context →
Dates, funding & sources

Two planned phases have different financing records and must remain separately identified.

  • Planned scope · June 17, 2026 Council report

    Phase I: 132 apartments. Phase II: 105. Combined planned scope: 237. At the June 17, 2026 Council action, the staff report described Phase I as under construction and forecast completion in February 2028.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-007

  • Phase I · reported financing closing

    $43.1 million reportedly closed in December 2025 after approval of up to $50 million. Neither is a City spending total.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-009

  • Phase II · June 17, 2026 authorization

    Up to $10 million from mixed funding sources and up to 54 project-based vouchers; execution and fund split remain unverified.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-010
Keep in mind

237 is the planned two-phase scope, not completed Phase I apartments. Approval, closing, assistance and payment figures are not interchangeable or additive. The February 2028 forecast reflects the June 2026 report. Current construction status and actual completion remain unverified.

Salvation Army family shelter expansion

Phoenix District 8, Maricopa County · Salvation Army family shelter expansion

  • Adopted City funding authority and site dedication

    Phoenix replaced the original $6 million CDBG and CDBG-CV commitment with up to $6 million in HOME-ARP in 2023. Council added up to $2 million in 2025, bringing the HOME-ARP authority to $8 million. The City announced the site dedication on May 12, 2026.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-030
Keep in mind

The initial CDBG commitment was replaced and cannot be added again. Funding authority does not establish cash paid. The ceremony marked the site for future construction. Opening, expenditures and people served by the expansion remain unverified. The December 3, 2025 Council report describes a $10 million total project cost and a loan. The May 12, 2026 City announcement describes an estimated $8 million project and a grant. The reviewed sources leave the differences unexplained. An executed agreement, any amendments and a reconciled budget are needed to establish the terms and cost.
