Two Phoenix housing projects: What the City certified

Phoenix certified that Phoenix Scholar House, a 56-home development, met the terms of its City HOME development loan agreement as of October 26, 2026. The certificate was recorded November 6, 2026. City certificate of completion, p. 1 (20260641371).

The City separately certified contractual completion for La Esperanza Terrace as of January 12, 2026. The certificate covers 96 homes and was recorded January 29, 2026. City certificate of completion, p. 1 (20260052973).

These certificates document completion under the respective City agreements. They do not establish final payments, HUD financial closeout, current occupancy or apartment availability.

Goodyear plans home repairs and legal help for renters

Goodyear’s 2026–27 action plan lists $323,561 for repairs to owner-occupied homes, $62,268 for legal services for renters facing unlawful eviction, and $96,457 for administration. Those three lines total $482,286. The plan covers July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. These figures are planned uses; they do not show how much was paid, whether services have started, or how many households got help. Read the City’s project breakdown.

Sources and method

HAP matched Arizona grantee names and HUD grantee keys to the regular CDBG column in the original annual formula allocation workbooks. The checked set contains 19 named places or grantees, 133 place/year checks and 101 direct HUD allocations. It is not an inventory of all CDBG-supported activity. Allocations are not adjusted for inflation, and do not include separately reported CDBG-CV or disaster funds.

Download the 133 checked source rows (CSV). Each positive row has a source workbook, sheet, cell and exact amount. A blank amount represents no separate row; never replace it with zero.

  • Proposed ground leases

    Tempe’s April 2024 Council agenda identified two 52-apartment financing portions, one using 4% and one using 9% Low-Income Housing Tax Credits. Together the proposal describes 104 apartments. The 19 for-sale townhomes are a separate component.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-029

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-029

Salvation Army family shelter expansion

Phoenix District 8, Maricopa County · Salvation Army family shelter expansion

  • Adopted City funding authority and site dedication

    Phoenix replaced the original $6 million CDBG and CDBG-CV commitment with up to $6 million in HOME-ARP in 2023. Council added up to $2 million in 2025, bringing the HOME-ARP authority to $8 million. The City announced the site dedication on May 12, 2026.

    Read the record · PHX-WEB-030
Keep in mind

The initial CDBG commitment was replaced and cannot be added again. Funding authority does not establish cash paid. The ceremony marked the site for future construction. Opening, expenditures and people served by the expansion remain unverified. The December 3, 2025 Council report describes a $10 million total project cost and a loan. The May 12, 2026 City announcement describes an estimated $8 million project and a grant. The reviewed sources leave the differences unexplained. An executed agreement, any amendments and a reconciled budget are needed to establish the terms and cost.
