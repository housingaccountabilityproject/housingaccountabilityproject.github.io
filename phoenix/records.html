About this index · November 8, 2026 snapshot

These are navigation counts from three published pages, not counts of projects, awards or homes. Evidence entries include grouped findings. Different source IDs can reference the same document or separate metadata snapshots.

This index reorganizes published research; it does not recheck external sources or establish current housing availability. Unknown amounts remain unknown.

Four records. Four different meanings.

These are separate measures, not a guaranteed sequence. Evidence of one does not establish the others.

  1. A

    Approved

    Permission or a ceiling.

    What does this establish?

    An adopted action or authorization shows what is permitted. It does not prove that an agreement was executed or money was paid.

    Moreland II's assistance authorization
  2. B

    Financing secured

    A financing event.

    What does this establish?

    A reported closing describes financing. Read the closing documents and the funder’s role before describing it as City spending.

    Moreland I's reported financing closing
  3. C

    Money spent

    A dated payment or expenditure.

    What does this establish?

    Look for disbursement or expenditure records matched to the program, project and period. A reported program total may not identify a specific project.

    Why an annual Capital Fund expenditure row needs context
  4. D

    Housing delivered

    A reported housing milestone.

    What does this establish?

    Read the completion or opening record with its date and evidence status. It does not by itself establish current occupancy or available vacancies.

    25th @ Bell's draft completion report

Public Housing Capital FundPHX-CFP-WEB-006

Capital Fund: obligation, draw and expenditure definitions

Program definitions

HUD program definitions · Not Phoenix transaction evidence

PHA obligation: a binding agreement for work or financing that will result in outlays.

Grant draw: Capital Funds vouchered through HUD’s Line of Credit Control System (LOCCS).

HUD grant-reporting expenditure: the PHA pays a contractor or vendor bill using funds disbursed from LOCCS.

Keep in mind

What this does not show A PHA obligation is different from HUD’s federal award obligation. This HUD grant-reporting definition of expenditure is distinct from the SEFA accounting measure. These definitions do not establish Phoenix’s amounts, dates or completion of grant-processing requirements.

Dates, scope & sources

Scope HUD Capital Fund guidance for public housing agencies (PHAs); national definitions, not a Phoenix ledger. Period and source date HUD training materials dated September 25, 2023.

Financial stage: Definitions separating a PHA commitment, a grant disbursement and expenditure.
