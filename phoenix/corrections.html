A correction preserves the record.

A supported entry keeps the earlier statement, corrected statement, evidence and publication date together. The empty log below does not establish that every finding is verified.

What belongs in this history?

When a supported correction changes a published finding, an entry preserves the earlier statement, the corrected statement, the reason, the evidence and the publication date. A layout improvement or an unexplained source discrepancy is not a correction.
