Maricopa County Property Waitlists

The Housing Authority of Maricopa County says applications for Casa Bonitas Apartments, Clare Feldstadt Apartments, Heritage at Surprise Apartments, and Heritage Senior Apartments close at 8 a.m. MST on December 1, 2026, until further notice. The closure covers all bedroom sizes at these properties, and paper applications will not be accepted. Check HAMC’s current schedule for how to apply and for property-specific limits that may take effect earlier. These are HAMC property lists, separate from City of Phoenix housing programs.

A waitlist is not a vacancy. Sources checked November 9, 2026; other resource links retain their earlier review date.

Housing navigation and shelter referrals · Surprise

Where to get shelter help in Surprise

Native American Connections runs the West Valley Housing Assistance Center at 12779 W. Grand Ave. in Surprise. It lists housing navigation and assessment help Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Phoenix Rescue Mission operates eight shelter units for families at the site but handles shelter eligibility and intake off-site. Families with children under 18 can call the Family Housing Hub at 877-211-8661. For single adults, the operator lists the Brian Garcia Welcome Center in Phoenix at 602-229-5155.

Check the operator’s current instructions before going. The center’s resource hours are not walk-in shelter intake hours, and eight units is not a vacancy count.

Federal shelter-definition reference · Phoenix & Maricopa County

What a motel stay can mean for shelter eligibility

Federal homelessness definitions generally include a motel stay paid for by a charity or government program as emergency shelter. Maricopa County’s future provider solicitation also allows hotel or motel vouchers when congregate emergency shelter is unavailable.

These records do not establish how a specific local intake office assesses a household, whether vouchers are available now, or whether a household will be placed.
