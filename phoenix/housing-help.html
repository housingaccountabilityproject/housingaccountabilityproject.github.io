Phoenix housing help

Start with official program information and established referral services.

Links and descriptions checked November 8, 2026 Arizona time (November 9 UTC). Eligibility, funding, intake status, appointments and vacancies may change. This project does not provide housing placements, determine eligibility or offer legal advice.

City government

Rent, utility and crisis assistance

Read about City crisis support, including rent and utility assistance, move-in support, case management and referrals.

Funding is limited. Program rules and appointment availability vary; the City must determine eligibility.

City government

Facing eviction

Review the City eviction legal-services program, its screening steps and options for legal representation, mediation or post-eviction help.

Services depend on program screening and eligibility. This link does not establish that assistance will be provided.

Landlord and tenant questions

Find City educational counseling about rental rights and responsibilities, common disputes and referrals.

The educational counseling service does not provide legal advice or representation. Separate emergency-assistance rules apply.

Homelessness resources

A listed shelter or resource is not a verified vacancy or placement offer. Contact the service for its current process.

211 Arizona referrals

Search statewide resources or call 211 for connections to housing, shelter, utility and other local services.
