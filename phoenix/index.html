How do funding records differ?

Four records. Four different meanings.

These are separate measures, not a guaranteed sequence. Evidence of one does not establish the others.

  1. A

    Approved

    Permission or a ceiling.

    What does this establish?

    An adopted action or authorization shows what is permitted. It does not prove that an agreement was executed or money was paid.

    Moreland II's assistance authorization
  2. B

    Financing secured

    A financing event.

    What does this establish?

    A reported closing describes financing. Read the closing documents and the funder’s role before describing it as City spending.

    Moreland I's reported financing closing
  3. C

    Money spent

    A dated payment or expenditure.

    What does this establish?

    Look for disbursement or expenditure records matched to the program, project and period. A reported program total may not identify a specific project.

    Why an annual Capital Fund expenditure row needs context
  4. D

    Housing delivered

    A reported housing milestone.

    What does this establish?

    Read the completion or opening record with its date and evidence status. It does not by itself establish current occupancy or available vacancies.

    25th @ Bell's draft completion report

What Phoenix’s 71,128 housing figure counts

Phoenix’s July–September 2026 Housing Phoenix Plan report lists 71,128 housing units created or preserved to date: 7,110 affordable units created, 6,363 affordable units preserved, 17,584 workforce units created and 40,071 market-rate units created. New-unit tracking begins in October 2019.

Scope: City of Phoenix, not the county or metro area. Report: July–September 2026; cumulative count through September. Creation data begin in October 2019; a common preservation start date is not specified. Source check: November 20, 2026.

Maricopa County proposes plan changes

Maricopa County · Proposed changes

Proposed amendment to the 2025–2029 Consolidated Plan

The County’s notice proposes Tempe leaving and Goodyear joining the HOME Consortium, and Buckeye leaving and Cave Creek joining the Urban County. It anticipates about 14% less HOME funding over three years and proposes reducing the HOME tenant-based rental-assistance goal from 650 to 500 households.

The notice proposes a $100,000 ESG outreach goal serving 150 people, with a $100,000 reduction in existing shelter operations and services goals. This is a proposed shift, not new net ESG funding.
