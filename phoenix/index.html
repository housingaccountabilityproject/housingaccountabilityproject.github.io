05 / What still needs answers?

Make the next
questions visible.

These are research questions, not findings of wrongdoing. Missing records or unresolved differences alone do not establish misuse or noncompliance.

01

Which assisted apartments overlap?

Unit-level schedules are needed before combining designations from different funders at the same property.

02

When do affordability restrictions end?

Use executed restriction agreements, amendments and their start dates. Leave unverified end dates blank.

03

What was actually paid?

Match project-specific disbursement records to the funder, phase and period before calculating spending per apartment.

04

How do conflicting records get resolved?

Keep both dated sources visible until a correction, executed agreement or closeout record explains the difference.
