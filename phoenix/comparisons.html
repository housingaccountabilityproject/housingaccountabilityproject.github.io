How the statements differ

The authorization describes 11 units at or below 65% AMI for at least 20 years. The later report describes 16 units at or below 40% AMI with the same $896,564 allocation.

An executed amendment or other explanatory record connecting the authorization to the performance statement.

How the statements differ

The master ACC amount of $2,842,753 is $28,849 higher than the separate formula amount of $2,813,904. This is arithmetic between published sources, not a verified award-revision transaction.

What remains unresolved

The reviewed records do not establish the transaction, date or reason linking these amounts. A file-update label is not a Phoenix transaction date. The difference is not evidence of missing money or a cash receipt.

Evidence needed

An explanatory transaction or revision record linking the published amounts, alongside grant-level approval and reporting records.

  • How do conflicting records get resolved?
  • HUD-approved grant Annual Statements/Budgets and approved five-year plan
  • Grant-level Performance and Evaluation or LOCCS reports
