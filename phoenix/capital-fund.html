Three terms worth separating

A commitment is
not a payment.

PHA means public housing agency. These are HUD program definitions, not evidence of a Phoenix transaction.

HUD program definitions · Not Phoenix transaction evidence

PHA obligation:

a binding agreement for work or financing that will result in outlays.

Grant draw:

Capital Funds vouchered through HUD’s Line of Credit Control System (LOCCS).

HUD grant-reporting expenditure:

the PHA pays a contractor or vendor bill using funds disbursed from LOCCS.

What this does not showA PHA obligation is different from HUD’s federal award obligation. This HUD grant-reporting definition of expenditure is distinct from the SEFA accounting measure. These definitions do not establish Phoenix’s amounts, dates or completion of grant-processing requirements.
ScopeHUD Capital Fund guidance for public housing agencies (PHAs); national definitions, not a Phoenix ledger.
Period and source dateHUD training materials dated September 25, 2023.

Financial stage: Definitions separating a PHA commitment, a grant disbursement and expenditure.

Follow the original record.

Official workbooks, draft City plans, the final Single Audit and HUD guidance serve different purposes. Their source IDs and exact page or cell locators stay attached.
