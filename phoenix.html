The Moreland: planned phases

The Moreland is planned as two phases totaling 237 apartments: 132 in Phase I and 105 in Phase II. At the June 17, 2026 Council action, the staff report described Phase I as under construction and forecast completion in February 2028.

Keep in mind. An older accomplishments summary labels Phase I with 237; do not use 237 as completed Phase I units. The February 2028 forecast reflects the June 2026 report. Current construction status and actual completion remain unverified.

From the published record

Moreland II: mixed-fund authorization and vouchers

On June 17, 2026, Council authorized up to $10 million from HOME, NSP and other affordable-housing funds, plus up to 54 project-based vouchers, for Moreland Phase II.

Keep in mind. No fund split or executed agreement was verified. Do not label all $10m HOME, spent or paid; up to 54 is not a verified count of executed PBV contracts.

Moreland I: authorization and reported bond closing

Published research update

Phoenix-area housing counts and shelter funding

The records distinguish 450 preserved Phoenix Manor apartments, 104 La Victoria Commons apartments in two financing portions plus separate townhomes, and up to $8 million in Salvation Army HOME-ARP authority.

Full context & qualifications

Occupancy and household outcomes, completed or occupied La Victoria homes, and shelter expansion payments, opening and people served require separate evidence.

The April 25, 2024 agenda describes proposed ground leases. Executed leases, financing closing, construction completion and occupancy require separate evidence.

The December 3, 2025 Council report describes a $10 million total project cost and a loan. The May 12, 2026 City announcement describes an estimated $8 million project and a grant. The reviewed sources leave the differences unexplained. An executed agreement, any amendments and a reconciled budget are needed to establish the terms and cost.
