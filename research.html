Research Overview

This library presents selected questions and preliminary analyses from an ongoing independent review of Tucson-area housing programs.

It is not a complete accounting of all local housing funding or a formal government audit. The research distinguishes reported facts, internal calculations, independently corroborated findings, and unresolved questions.

Publication status: Numerical summaries below are drawn from the project's internal research workbook. Original government documents and page-level citations require final verification before these summaries are treated as independently substantiated findings.

Funding and Financial Reconciliation

Figures below concern the historical PY2024 reporting period unless otherwise noted. They do not represent current cash balances, uncommitted funds, or presently available housing assistance.

CDBG — Program Income

The research workbook identifies $349,468 in CDBG program income included within a reported current-resource subtotal of $5,418,132.

That program income should not be added to the subtotal a second time.

Internal references: FUND-001, FUND-003, VAR-001, SRC-023; PY2024 CAPER pp. 13 and 149.

HOME — Resources and Expenditures

The workbook records approximately $3,148,083 in reported resources and $479,243 in expenditures for PY2024.

The difference cannot be interpreted automatically as unspent, unobligated, or available funding. Commitments, activity timing, and accounting definitions must be examined.

Internal references: FUND-004, FUND-005, SRC-023; PY2024 CAPER pp. 13 and 39–40.

ESG — Rapid Rehousing

The research workbook identifies a PY2024 rapid-rehousing performance comparison of 30 planned and 18 actual.

Additional records are needed to understand the reporting population, measurement method, and reasons for the difference.

Internal references: FQ-004 and SRC-023; PY2024 CAPER p. 9.

Continuum of Care Funding

The research examines federal Continuum of Care awards and their relationship to provider agreements, project operations, services delivered, and housing outcomes.

A funding award does not establish that a particular housing unit is currently vacant or available to an applicant.

Next verification: Match award records with the correct grant periods and implementation reports.

Research and Publication Standards

Evidence Classification

Distinguish official agency statements, independently verified facts, project calculations, interpretations, and unresolved questions.

Financial Comparability

Compare matching periods and categories. Do not combine awards, obligations, expenditures, program income, and available balances without appropriate reconciliation.

Fair Representation

Recognize documented progress and agency explanations. Missing records or reporting differences alone do not establish misconduct or noncompliance.

Corrections and Privacy

Correct material errors with dated explanations. Exclude private case records, medical information, personal identifiers, and sensitive correspondence unless expressly authorized after review.
