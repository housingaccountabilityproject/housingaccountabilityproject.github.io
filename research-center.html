F-001 · CDBG · PY2026

$1.5 Million CDBG Reallocation

The City reported approving a shift of existing planned CDBG funding from facilities toward eligible housing activities in October 2026. It was not $1.5 million in new federal funding.

F-007 · formula CDBG · PY2024

What Still Needs Explaining in CDBG Spending?

The corrected PR-26 reports $4,717,936.37 in formula-CDBG expenditures. The City’s narrative reports $3,433,346.66, leaving a $1,284,589.71 arithmetic difference requiring a transaction-level explanation.

Outstanding: Match activities and dates within formula CDBG. OMB guidance separates CDBG-CV reporting; the old mixed-program comparison is withdrawn.

This is not a finding of missing funds, currently available assistance or a cost per housing outcome. CDBG-CV is reported separately.

F-003 · PBV · August 2026

Craycroft and Lee Street Voucher Allocations

The City's notice identifies 82 allocated Section 8 project-based vouchers across 74 existing Craycroft and eight Lee Street apartments.

Meaning: 82 allocations do not establish 82 vacancies, signed HAP contracts, or applicant access.

Public contract status, aggregate occupancy counts and published referral rules still need verification.

Draft Versus Final Performance Reporting
