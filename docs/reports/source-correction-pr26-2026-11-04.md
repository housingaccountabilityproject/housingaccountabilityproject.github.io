1. Rounded CR-15 expenditure amount minus CDBG and CDBG-CV narrative amounts: $4,717,936.00 - $3,433,346.66 - $339,616.74 = **$944,972.60**.

Both arithmetic differences are directly reproducible from the now-located source figures. **Neither is a verified shortfall, improper spending, missing money, or independently reconciled accounting variance**, because the figures may reflect different scopes and periods.
