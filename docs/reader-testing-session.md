# Reader session handout

The website is being tested, not you. Explore it as you normally would. You can skip a task or stop at any point. Please do not share personal housing experiences, application details or resident information. No account or personal information is needed.

## Tasks for a participant

1. Start on the homepage. In your own words, what does the site help you learn? Choose a research area and somewhere you would begin.
2. Find a project that interests you. Explain where it is, what stage it has reached and what the housing number describes.
3. Someone says a funding approval shows how much the City spent. Use the site to explain what you would say and what evidence you would check.
4. Find the original source behind a claim. Explain where in that document you would look and which date matters.
5. Find a question the existing research cannot answer. Explain what additional evidence could help.
6. Search the city's records, prepare a printable set of results, and return to the page you were using.
7. Switch to the other research area and find the same kind of information. Explain what feels familiar and what differs.

After each task: What was confusing? How sure are you about your interpretation? At the end: What would make this guide easier to use?
