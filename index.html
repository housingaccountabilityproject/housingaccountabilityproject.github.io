Our Research Methodology

Our research follows a structured evidence and quality-control process.

Traceable Evidence

Identify source documents, reporting dates, jurisdictions, and applicable program periods.

Comparable Financial Data

Compare matching funding categories and time periods, distinguishing awards, obligations, payments, and expenditures.

Fair and Correctable Findings

Recognize documented achievements, include relevant agency explanations, identify limitations, and correct errors when verified.

Publication policy: Private case records, personal identifiers, and sensitive correspondence are excluded from public releases unless separately reviewed and expressly authorized for publication.

THE STORY BEHIND THE RESEARCH

From Personal Experience to Public Accountability

Founded by William Connell, the Housing Accountability Project began with firsthand questions about housing assistance in Tucson. What began as an effort to understand an unsuccessful housing opportunity grew into an independent, nonpartisan initiative examining public housing funding, transparency, and outcomes.

The project was born from personal experience, but its mission is much larger.
