# Housing Accountability Project — Approved Brand Assets

Brand direction: **Evidence & Accountability (Option A)**, approved November 3, 2026.

## Brand identity
- Full name: **Housing Accountability Project**. Prefer the full name; HAP also means Housing Assistance Payments in HUD terminology.
- Tagline: **Follow the funding. Understand the outcomes.**
- Descriptor: Independent, nonpartisan, public-interest research. Never imply that the project is an official government agency, a registered nonprofit, or a provider of housing placements.
- Visual theme: research document, rising data bars, magnifying glass, house and subtle Tucson-inspired desert imagery.

## Approved colors
- Navy: `#102e3d` — headers, primary grounds and wordmark
- Teal: `#166267` — research accents, navigation
- Mint: `#b9e9db` — highlights and legibility
- Off-white: `#f7faf9` — backgrounds, whitespace

## Asset reference
- `logo-mark.svg`: scalable emblem for headers and icons
- `logo-lockup-light.svg` and `logo-lockup-dark.svg`: full-wordmark variants
- `social-preview.svg`: editable 1200 × 630 layout
- `social-preview.png`: Facebook, LinkedIn and other social card preview (1200 × 630)
- `logo-mark-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png`: raster exports

The PNG files are generated from versioned SVGs by `.github/workflows/render-brand-images.yml`. Modify the SVG sources, not generated PNGs.
